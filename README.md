Thank you for using our application.
This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles user information.

1. Information We Collect
We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from users.
All QR code and barcode scanning activities are processed entirely on your device. We do not send any data to external servers.

2. Permissions We Use
Camera Permission
Our application requires access to your device’s camera solely for:
Scanning QR codes and barcodes
Storage & Gallery
We access your photo library only when you explicitly use the "Scan from Gallery" feature or when you choose to save a generated QR code image to your device.
Local Storage (Hive)
All data, including your scan history, generation history, and favorites, are stored locally on your device. This information is never uploaded to our servers or synchronized with any cloud service.
We store local performance logs (such as ad display status and technical success/failure events) on your device to help diagnose technical issues. This data remains on your device and is not shared with us or third parties.

3. Data Sharing

4. Third-Party Services

5. Data Security
Since we do not collect or store user data, there is no risk of data being accessed or shared from our systems.
