Key Business Features

🛍️ E-Commerce Core

Product catalog, variants, related products, search & filter, inventory display, and geographic shipping zones.

📰 Content Management

Blog/news system, media gallery, dynamic pages, landing page builder, and rich text editing with CKEditor.

👥 Admin Management

Multi-user admin system, role-based access control, analytics dashboard, and bulk operations.

🌍 Globalization

Multi-language support, geographic data hierarchy, language switching, and translation management.

Contact forms, newsletter subscriptions, and email notifications via Postmark/AWS SES.

Visitor analytics, cache management, system monitoring, and SEO optimization.

Secure admin access with token-based authentication, auto-refresh, and role-based access control (RBAC).

Microservices Architecture

Headless frontend consuming separate backend APIs, enabling independent scaling and multi-tenant support.
